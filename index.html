Hermes Gmail

A private, owner-operated email assistant for searching and reviewing an authorized Gmail mailbox.

What it does

The assistant helps the account owner find messages, read email and attachments, summarize conversations, and prepare reply text for review.

Read-only access

Google authorization grants read-only Gmail access and basic account identity information. This integration cannot send mail, change messages or labels, or delete email. Access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Personal use

This application is intended for its operator's personal use. It is not a public email service and does not offer public sign-up. This website provides application information only; it does not display or collect mailbox contents or host a login form.

Email content selected for a task may be processed by the operator's configured AI service and communication platform. See the privacy policy before authorizing access.
