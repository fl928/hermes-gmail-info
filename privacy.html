Privacy policy

This policy describes Hermes Gmail, a private, owner-operated email assistant. It is not a public service and has no public registration.

Information accessed

After the account owner authorizes Google OAuth, the integration can access Gmail messages, headers, labels, settings and attachments within the granted read-only scope. Basic Google account identity information is used to identify the authorized account. The integration does not request the account password.

Purpose and permissions

Information is used to perform email tasks requested by the account owner, including search, reading, summarization and preparation of reply text. Read-only access does not permit sending, changing or deleting mail.

Processing and disclosure

Selected email content and attachments may be transmitted to AI providers configured by the operator to perform requested tasks. Requests and responses may also be processed and retained by the communication platform used to interact with the assistant. Those providers' policies and the operator's configuration govern their processing and retention. Do not assume that mailbox data remains entirely on the operator's device.

The operator does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google user data must be handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The operator is responsible for choosing provider settings consistent with these commitments before processing mailbox content.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted local storage with restricted file permissions. Retrieved content and generated outputs may remain in local files, conversation history, logs, memory or backups, and in configured providers' systems. No automatic deletion period is promised. The operator controls local retention and must separately manage retention or deletion with external providers.

Revocation and deletion

The account owner can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not delete previously stored copies. To remove stored data, the operator must delete relevant local credentials, caches, exports, conversation history and memory, and request deletion from applicable external services where available.

Website and privacy questions

This informational website embeds no analytics, tracking scripts, forms or third-party resources. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection information to deliver the pages. No mailbox contents or credentials are published on this website. As this is a self-operated application, privacy requests and configuration changes are managed directly by the account owner through the assistant's existing private control channel; do not post private information in public repository issues.
